Frequently Asked Questions

What is Netflix?

Netflix is a streaming service that offers a wide range of award-winning TV shows, films, documentaries and original content across a variety of genres and languages.

Where is Netflix available?

Netflix is available in most regions worldwide. Availability of specific titles can vary by country due to licensing agreements.

How do I cancel?

Netflix subscriptions can be cancelled at any time from the account settings page. You keep access until the end of the current billing period.

How do I change my password?

Open the account page, choose "Change password", verify your current password and set a new one. Passwords must be at least six characters long.

What is the cheapest tier?

The Basic plan is the lowest priced option and includes HD streaming on a single screen at a time, with downloads available on mobile devices.

Can I share my account?

Household members can use the same account and create their own profiles, each with separate viewing history and preferences.
